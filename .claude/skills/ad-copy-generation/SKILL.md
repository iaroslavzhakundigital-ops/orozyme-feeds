# Ad Copy Generation

Part of [Agent Skills™](https://github.com/itallstartedwithaidea/agent-skills) by [googleadsagent.ai™](https://googleadsagent.ai)

## Description

Produces high-performance responsive search ad (RSA) copy. Generates headlines (up to 15, max 30 characters each) and descriptions (up to 4, max 90 characters each), optimizing for ad strength, CTR, and conversion relevance while adhering to Google Ads editorial policies.

## Use When

- User asks to "write ad copy" or "generate ads"
- User wants "new headlines" or "new descriptions" for RSAs
- User mentions "ad strength" improvement
- User asks for "A/B test variants" or "ad testing"
- User wants to "improve click-through rate" on ads

## Best Practices

1. Always provide at least 10 unique headlines
2. Include at least one headline with a clear call-to-action
3. Test at least 3 ad variants per ad group
4. Wait for statistical significance before pausing underperformers
5. Refresh ad copy quarterly to combat ad fatigue
