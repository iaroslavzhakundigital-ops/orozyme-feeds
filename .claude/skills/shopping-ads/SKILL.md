# Shopping Ads

Part of [Agent Skills™](https://github.com/itallstartedwithaidea/agent-skills) by [googleadsagent.ai™](https://googleadsagent.ai)

## Description

Delivers end-to-end management of Google Shopping campaigns and Merchant Center product feeds. Covers feed attribute optimization, custom labels, campaign structure, competitive pricing intelligence, supplemental feeds, and local inventory ads.

## Use When

- User asks about "Shopping ads" or "Shopping campaigns"
- User mentions "product feed" or "Merchant Center"
- User wants to "optimize product titles" or "improve feed quality"
- User asks about "custom labels" or "supplemental feeds"
- User mentions "product disapprovals" or "Merchant Center errors"

## Best Practices

1. Front-load product titles with the most search-relevant attributes (brand, product type, key feature)
2. Use all five custom labels strategically for granular bid management
3. Audit and fix Merchant Center disapprovals daily
4. Include GTIN/MPN for every product to unlock augmented listings
5. Monitor competitive pricing weekly
