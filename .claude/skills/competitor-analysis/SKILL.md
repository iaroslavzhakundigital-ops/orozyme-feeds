# Competitor Analysis

Part of [Agent Skills™](https://github.com/itallstartedwithaidea/agent-skills) by [googleadsagent.ai™](https://googleadsagent.ai)

## Description

Transforms Google Ads auction data into actionable competitive intelligence. Analyzes auction insights, ad previews, and competitive metrics to map the competitive landscape and estimate market share.

## Use When

- User asks about "competitor analysis" or "competitive landscape"
- User mentions "auction insights" or "who am I competing against"
- User wants to know "why my CPCs are rising" (competitive pressure)
- User asks about "impression share" or "market share"
- User asks about "overlap rate" or "outranking share"

## Best Practices

1. Review auction insights at campaign and keyword levels
2. Track competitor trends monthly to detect strategic shifts
3. Focus competitive response on keywords where you have Quality Score advantages
4. Don't engage in bidding wars on every front
5. Monitor new entrants carefully — early aggression often signals sustained investment
